Key tables used by this project

Customer (Area "Info")
  Fields: CustNum (int), Name, Address, Address2, City, State, Country,
          PostalCode, Contact, Phone, SalesRep, CreditLimit (dec),
          Balance (dec), Terms, Discount (int), Comments, Fax, EmailAddress
  Primary index: CustNum (unique, on CustNum)

Order (Area "Order")
  Fields: Ordernum (int), CustNum (int, FK to Customer), OrderDate,
          ShipDate, PromiseDate, Carrier, Instructions, PO, Terms, SalesRep,
          BillToID (int), ShipToID (int), OrderStatus, WarehouseNum (int),
          Creditcard
  Indexes: OrderNum (unique, primary), CustOrder (unique: CustNum + Ordernum),
           OrderDate, OrderStatus, SalesRep
  Note: OrderLine records link to a customer indirectly via Order.CustNum.

OrderLine (Area "Order")
  Fields: Ordernum (int, FK to Order), Linenum (int), Itemnum (int, FK to
          Item), Price (dec), Qty (int), Discount (int), ExtendedPrice (dec),
          OrderLineStatus

Item (Area "Inventory")
  Fields: Itemnum (int), ItemName, CatPage (int), Price (dec),
          CatDescription, Onhand (int), Allocated (int), ReOrder (int),
          OnOrder (int), Category1, Category2, Special, Weight (dec),
          Minqty (int)
  Indexes: ItemNum (unique, primary), CatDescription (word),
           Category2ItemName, CategoryItemName, ItemName (word)
